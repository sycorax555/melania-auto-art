# Automotive Canvas

Build an ultra-luxurious, modern, minimalist single-page portfolio website for a fine artist specializing in bespoke, high-end oil paintings of exotic and classic automobiles, fine artist London Melania. The design must perfectly match a sleek, high-contrast black-and-white minimalist business card aesthetic. The target audience includes ultra-high-net-worth car collectors, luxury dealership owners, and fine art enthusiasts.



Design Aesthetic & Theme (Strictly Black & White Minimalist):

- Color Palette: Pure matte black background (#000000 or #0A0A0A), stark crisp white text (#FFFFFF), and subtle silver/cool gray accents (#888888) for fine borders and secondary text. NO gold or bright colors.

- Aesthetic: Modern, moody, high-contrast, ultra-clean, and minimalist—resembling a high-end contemporary art gallery or resembling a private art gallery or a luxury supercar boutique rather than a standard web shop.



- Typography: Clean, widely-spaced, high-fashion sans-serif typography matching the business card style. Elegant serif headers for titles and clean, modern sans-serif body text for high readability.

   - Brand Text Subtitle: "AUTOMOTIVE ARTIST"

   - Right Nav Links: "Collection", "About", "Commission Process", "Inquire".

Gallery Section ("The Collection") (I will provide all images for this part as well)
